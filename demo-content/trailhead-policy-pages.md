## Page: Shipping Policy

We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally yet.

**Processing time:** Orders placed before 1 p.m. Eastern ship the same business day. Orders after 1 p.m. or on weekends ship the next business day.

**Shipping options (contiguous US):**
- Standard (3–5 business days): $6.95, free on orders over $75
- Expedited (2 business days): $14.95
- Overnight (next business day): $29.95, order by 11 a.m. Eastern

**Alaska and Hawaii:** Standard only, 5–8 business days, $12.95 flat. Free shipping does not apply.

**PO boxes and APO/FPO:** Standard shipping only.

**Tracking:** You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

**Lost or damaged packages:** Email us within 7 days of the delivery date with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or a refund.

## Page: Returns & Refunds

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery**.

**What can be returned:**
- Unused items in original packaging with tags attached: full refund
- Items used once or twice outdoors (for example a jacket worn on one hike) in clean, resellable condition: store credit only, not a refund
- Items with visible wear, damage, or missing parts: not returnable

**Final sale (cannot be returned):** sale items marked "Final Sale", opened water filters, socks and underwear, and gift cards.

**How to return:**
1. Email support@trailheadsupply.example with your order number and the item.
2. We reply within 1 business day with a prepaid return label.
3. Return shipping costs $6.95, deducted from your refund. Exchanges for a different size ship free.

**Refund timing:** Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item. Your bank may take 3–5 more days.

**Defective items:** Anything that fails from a manufacturing defect within 1 year is replaced free, including shipping.

## Page: FAQ

**Do you price match?**
No. We do not price match or offer discount codes through chat. Join our email list for occasional sales.

**How do I pick a jacket size?**
Our jackets run true to size. If you are between sizes or plan to layer a fleece underneath, size up.

**Can I change or cancel my order?**
Yes, within 1 hour of ordering. Email us right away. After that the order may already be packed.

**Do you sell gift cards?**
Yes, digital gift cards from $25 to $200. They never expire.

**Where are you located?**
We are an online-only store based in Denver, Colorado. There is no physical store for pickup.

**How do I contact a person?**
Email support@trailheadsupply.example. We reply within 1 business day, Monday to Friday.
